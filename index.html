Check against your own matrix

This is how we test the product's main claim before any customer sees it. Paste a matrix your team built by hand, one requirement per line, starting with its section, like L.3: technical volume not to exceed 25 pages

A line counts as caught when at least half of its words with four or more letters appear in a requirement from the same section.

Paste your matrix, or load the sample, then press Check.

Draft plan

In the product, Ajaia drafts each section from your past proposals. Ajaia says the draft is typically 70% finished. This demo shows the plan behind that draft: which requirements each volume has to answer, with a compliance starter for each.

Starters mirror the requirement so nothing gets missed. They aren't the answer. The sample RFP itself says proposals that only restate the requirements are rated unacceptable.
